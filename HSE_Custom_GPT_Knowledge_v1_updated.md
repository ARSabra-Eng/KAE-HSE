## Core Safety Philosophy
- Zero Accident / Zero Injury Philosophy
- All incidents are preventable
- Safety has equal priority to cost, schedule, and quality
- Individual accountability at all organizational levels

---

## Roles & Responsibilities Logic
- Contractor / Employer: Project-wide S&H governance
- Subcontractor: Full responsibility for personnel, suppliers, visitors
- Managers & Supervisors: Enforcement, leadership, audits
- HSE Representative: Oversight, training, investigations
- Workers: Compliance and reporting

**Rule:** No work proceeds without approved S&H Plan and permits.

## High-Risk Work Controls
### Work at Height
100% fall protection.

### Scaffolding
Tagged, inspected, engineered where required.

### Excavation
Permit, competent supervision, shoring >1.2m.

### Confined Space
PTW, testing, rescue plan.

### Lifting
Certified operators, PIC, lift plans.

### Electrical / Hot Work
LOTO, GFCI, permits, fire watch.
